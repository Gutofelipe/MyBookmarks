Privacy Policy — My Bookmarks

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Summary: My Bookmarks runs 100% locally on your device. It does not collect, log, sell, or transmit your bookmarks, URLs, or personal data to external servers.

1. Data Access and Processing

The My Bookmarks Google Chrome extension accesses your browser's saved bookmarks solely to display them in a hierarchical folder tree, enable instant search, allow creating, renaming, editing, and deleting folders and links, and let you organize items via drag-and-drop directly inside the extension interface.

2. 100% On-Device Artificial Intelligence

Optional semantic search and folder organization suggestions use Google Chrome's built-in local AI engine (Gemini Nano). All AI processing runs 100% locally on your computer. No bookmarks, URLs, titles, notes, tags, or personal data are ever sent to external servers, cloud APIs, or third parties.

3. Local Storage Only

Your interface preferences (selected language and expanded folders), custom bookmark #tags, personal notes, pinned flags, and temporary Undo history (for reverting deletions, moves, or AI folder organization) are stored exclusively on your own device using local browser storage (chrome.storage.local).

4. Permissions Scope

  • Bookmarks & Favicons: Used to display, search, edit, categorize, and show website icons for your saved bookmarks.
  • Storage: Used to store your local preferences, #tags, personal notes, and Undo snapshots on your device.
  • Tabs & Tab Groups: Used to check whether the active tab is already bookmarked, bookmark your active tab or open window tabs when requested, and open a bookmark folder as a Chrome Tab Group.
  • Context Menus & Side Panel: Used to provide right-click bookmarking shortcuts and an optional persistent Side Panel view.

5. Broken Link Check (User-Initiated)

Only when you manually click Check broken links in the Cleanup tab does the extension send lightweight HEAD requests directly to your saved bookmark URLs solely to check whether those pages respond. No browsing history or analytics are collected or sent to any third party.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

My Bookmarks does not use analytics, tracking scripts, advertisements, or third-party data processors. We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.
